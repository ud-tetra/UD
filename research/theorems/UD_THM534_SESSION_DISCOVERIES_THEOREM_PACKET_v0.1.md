## THM-534-R1 — Conditional six-channel stochastic spectrum [EXACT CONDITIONAL]
**Objects.** Let E be a six-element set partitioned into three disjoint opposite pairs. Let V=R^E with its standard Euclidean inner product. Define T:V→V by
```
T_{ij} = 1/2   if i=j;
       = 1/6   if j is opposite i;
       = 1/12  otherwise.
```
**Conclusion.** T is symmetric, entrywise nonnegative and doubly stochastic; its spectrum is
`{1 (×1), 1/2 (×2), 1/3 (×3)}`.
**Proof.** Every row sums to 1/2+1/6+4/12=1. Symmetry gives column sums. Let S swap each opposite pair, P±=(I±S)/2. On the antisymmetric pair sector T=(1/2−1/6)I_3=(1/3)I_3. On the symmetric pair sector T=(1/2)I_3+(1/6)J_3, giving 1 on the all-ones direction and 1/2 on its two-dimensional orthogonal complement. QED.
**Dependency firewall.** This proves the spectrum **given** the three transition weights. It does **not** derive 1/2, 1/6, 1/12 from tetrahedral geometry, event counts, a sampling measure, a transport generator or a renewal rule. The source-complete original THM-534 remains **SOURCE-BLOCKED**.
