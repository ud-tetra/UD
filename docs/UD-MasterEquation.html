The Field

The field is the zero. Not empty — structured. Not absent — balanced.
DEFINITION 0n := 𝒵n The zero at level n is a structured object carrying the full state of the cascade.

Unified Dynamics begins not with a particle, not with a field in the usual sense, but with a structured zero — a state of balanced signed equilibrium between inflow and outflow.

GROUND STATE 𝒵0 = 0 Balanced signed equilibrium. No structure. The state from which all distinction emerges.

A perturbation Δn disturbs the zero. The perturbation equals the directional flow across the boundary between interior and exterior:

Nn(in/out) = 0 + Δn N = 0 → balance. N ≠ 0 → difference. The perturbation IS the inflow/outflow imbalance.

The branch Bn = sgn(Δn) determines the character of the perturbation: repair (−1), balance (0), or formation (+1). The system responds by measuring two quantities — agreement and coherence — that determine whether the perturbation survives.

The Operator

Sense. Decide. Update. The same operator at every level.
MASTER EQUATION 𝒵n+1 = Ω0(𝒵n, Δn; Ξn) The next level's zero is produced by the master operator acting on the current zero, the perturbation, and the context.

The master operator decomposes into three stages:

Ω0 = 𝒰0 ∘ Φ0 ∘ Ψ0 Ψ₀ senses. Φ₀ decides. 𝒰₀ updates. Applied right to left.

Ψ0 — The Sensory Evaluation (six-fold test)

Ψ0 = ΨA ∧ ΨP ∧ ΨB ∧ ΨC ∧ Ψ𝓑 ∧ ΨR

  • ΨA — Agreement: is the inflow/outflow balanced?
  • ΨP — Perturbation: is the disturbance within bounds?
  • ΨB — Branch: is the direction well-defined?
  • ΨC — Coherence: is the system stable under perturbation?
  • Ψ𝓑 — Burden: is the load manageable?
  • ΨR — Reserve: does healing capacity exist?

All six must pass (conjunction). This is the evaluation gate.

Φ0 — The Decision Function

Φ0 : (Bn, Ψ0, 𝓑n, ℛn) ↦ (Dn, ρn, Πn)

Maps branch, sensory result, burden, and reserve into a decision D, confidence ρ, and promotion payload Π.

The decision Dn takes one of six values:

HoldPerturbation noted. Zero unchanged. No action.
RepairInternal adjustment. Zero preserved. Healing activated.
PromotePerturbation passes. Zero advances by Πn.
RelayPromoteCascade of sub-promotions. Zero advances by Σ Πn,j.
DeferViable but mistimed. Zero unchanged. Queued.
RejectEvaluation failed. Zero unchanged. Perturbation expelled.

𝒰0 — The Update Rule

Only Promote and RelayPromote change the zero. All other decisions preserve it. Most perturbations are filtered. The cascade advances only through the narrowest gate.

The Cascade

Balance → Difference → Evaluation → Agreement → Coherence.
0 → Δ → B → A → C Ground → perturbation → branch → agreement → coherence. Five stages. One direction.

Each cascade stage maps to a specific structural level of the simplicial complex that emerges at the fixed point:

Stage 0: Balance

𝒵0 = 0. No structure. Pre-distinction ground. The structured zero before any perturbation.

Stage 1: Difference

Δ ≠ 0. Vertices emerge — V = 4 points of imbalance. Each vertex is a site where the zero is disturbed. The (in/out) direction gives each vertex a sign.

Stage 2: Evaluation

The identity gate. Ψ0 tests the perturbation. The system must recognize itself as a system (+1 in the support weight). Without self-recognition, the distinction collapses.

Stage 3: Agreement

E = 6 edges form (pairwise differences). F = 4 faces close (triadic agreements). Local compatibility of evaluated differences. The agreement threshold Ac = (d−1)/d = 2/3.

Stage 4: Coherence

All agreements hold simultaneously. The body closes. K4 = the tetrahedron. The first 3-dimensional closed structure where every local agreement is globally compatible.

The cascade produces 13 = V+E+F−1 transition levels between ground and coherence. This number appears in the fine structure constant: αEM−1 = 13π².

The Fixed Point

The tetrahedron is not assumed. It is derived as the unique terminal state of the cascade.
FIXED POINT THEOREM 𝒵* = K4 K₄ is the unique fixed point of Ω₀ subject to nontrivial coherence, physical dimension, spectral identity, and topological closure.

Proof. Four conditions, each independently necessary:

Condition (i): Nontrivial coherence

F > 1 (more than one face). Local agreement must be distinguishable from global coherence. Requires V ≥ 4.

Condition (ii): Physical dimension

d = V−1 = 3 (three spatial dimensions). The first dimension where an interior exists. Requires V = 4.

Condition (iii): Spectral identity

⟨|Eface|⟩ = E/V. The average absolute face magnetization equals the edge-to-vertex ratio. Holds for V = 3 (trivially) and V = 4. Fails for V ≥ 5. K₄-specific.

Condition (iv): Topological closure

χ = V−E+F = 2. Closed orientable surface. Gauss-Bonnet curvature: 2πχ = 4π. Holds for all simplicial spheres.

Conjunction of (i)–(iv): V = 4 uniquely. ∎

At the fixed point, no perturbation can advance 𝒵* further. Every evaluation threshold is saturated with zero margin. The tetrahedron is the tightest possible structure that passes all six sensory tests simultaneously.
