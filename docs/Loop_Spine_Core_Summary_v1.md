**R(ψ, n)** — Retention fidelity (THM-LS1)
Squared cosine of angle between ψ and T^n ψ. Standard QI fidelity. Basis-independent. R = 1 for all T eigenstates at all n.

**B_repair(ψ, δψ)** — Repair burden, energy form (THM-LS2)
Energy cost of projecting a perturbed state back to its eigenspace: B = Σ_{μ≠λ} (1−μ) ‖P_μ δψ‖². Measures the energy content of the out-of-eigenspace perturbation component. State-distance formulation retired.

**B_repair = 0 in degenerate sectors** (THM-LS3)
If δψ ∈ V_λ (perturbation within the eigenspace), then P_μ δψ = 0 for all μ ≠ λ, so B = 0. Applied to P_-: the 3D color sector (T = (1/3)I₃) has zero energy cost for all internal perturbations.

**P_λ** — Spectral restore projector (THM-LS4)
Orthogonal projector onto the T eigenspace V_λ. Uniquely defined by T via the spectral theorem. Basis-independent.

**L_T = C⁶** — Transport totality (THM-LS5)
All T eigenvalues are strictly positive (min = 1/3). No nonzero state is annihilated by transport. Every channel configuration propagates.

**Δλ_min = 1/6** — Minimum spectral gap (THM-LS6)
Attained between λ = 1/2 and λ = 1/3. The smallest energy difference between distinct T eigenspaces under H = I − T.
