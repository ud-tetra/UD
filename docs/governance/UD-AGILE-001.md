# UD-AGILE-001 — Agile Hypothesis and Independent Derivation Protocol
Date: 2026-11-11
Status: GOVERNANCE BASELINE (revisable by versioned amendment)
Physical Promotion: 0
Scope: All future UD mathematical, discovery, bridge, comparison and publication work.

## Mission and scientific boundary
UD seeks a falsifiable mathematical account of phenomena in observable three-dimensional space. The filled K4 3-simplex is a minimal simplicial carrier, not a proven physical substrate. Primitive/higher dimensions are optional mathematical extensions, not required ontological claims. Physical claims require observable, dimensional and normalization bridge, target-independent prediction, uncertainty and specified failure criteria.

## Agile hypothesis lifecycle
1. REGISTER: Give each hypothesis an ID/version, precise statement, source revision, domain, assumptions, motivation, and prior target exposure.
2. DERIVE: Derive consequences from admitted UD primitives independently. External ideas may inspire a question, but their conclusions, constants, fitted factors, or unexplained maps may not be silently imported as UD derivations.
3. CHALLENGE: Attempt counterexamples, no-go results, competing models, sensitivity checks, and independent replay. Document negative outcomes.
4. TEST: Predeclare observables, null/alternative hypotheses, units, scales, uncertainty, selection rules, held-out data and failure thresholds before accessing evaluation targets.
5. DECIDE: RETAIN / REVISE / REJECT / HOLD with evidence and explicit downstream dependency impacts. Retain prior versions and rejected branches.
6. PROMOTE: Physical promotion requires separately audited empirical correspondence and independently testable predictions. Until then promotion = 0.

## External-source firewall
- Gibsonian Mathematics and other outside frameworks are sources of questions or possible techniques, not authorities for UD physical claims.
- Quote/attribute primary sources accurately. Never assert a particular author's lack of testing, curve fitting, or constant insertion without a documented equation, source and evidence audit.
- Mark any reused external technique as INSPIRED_BY, and independently rederive it using declared UD premises; disclose any irreducible imported assumption.
- Maintain an input dependency ledger distinguishing STRUCTURAL / ASSUMED / EMPIRICAL / FITTED / TARGET_SELECTED / DERIVED.
- Sandbox target-guided exploration may proceed under FIT, but must never be described as target-blind derivation or auto-exported to mainline.

## Claim types
EXACT (within defined mathematical model); DERIVED/CONDITIONAL; HYPOTHESIS/CANDIDATE; FIT; NUMERICAL; EMPIRICAL; NO-GO; OPEN. An EXACT identity does not establish physical correspondence. A matching number does not certify a mechanism.

## Required hypothesis receipt
ID, version, owner/session, repo commit, statement, domain, source citations, admitted primitives, assumptions, dimensional and normalization conventions, target exposure, derivation steps, computational checks, counterexamples, alternative hypotheses, physical observable and measurement protocol, falsifier, current label, decision, dependency links, next test.

## Implementation rules
- Load this policy at boot and include its ID in every substantive research handoff.
- No retroactive relabeling of FIT as DERIVED. New assumptions require a new hypothesis version and dependent claims must be reevaluated.
- Do not claim an experiment occurred without instrument/data provenance and independent verification.
- Prefer exact rational/symbolic calculations; keep measurement decimals and uncertainties in their empirical domain.
- Use branch/claim/PR workflow and repository validation; governance commits alone do not certify physics.

## Mission-first external ideas rule (amendment 2026-11-11)
**Primary objective: establish, test and where warranted support Unified Dynamics, not disprove Gibsonian Mathematics or any competing framework.** A competitor critique is ancillary and must be evidence-led, proportionate and relevant to a UD derivation or test. Do not frame the research program as a campaign against an author.

Gibsonian Mathematics may supply conceptual inspiration or suggest mathematical questions. Such ideas may enter UD only as explicitly attributed INSPIRED_BY hypotheses, provided they are compatible with declared UD primitives or their incompatibility is openly investigated. Acceptance requires an independent derivation from UD dynamics, reproducible proof or computation within the stated model, and (for physical claims) a separately falsifiable observable bridge. No external numerical conclusion, fitted coefficient, or physical identification is inherited merely because an idea is interesting. Conceptual alignment is necessary for adoption but not sufficient for physical promotion.

When comparing frameworks, apply symmetrical claim-boundary standards and do not assert unsupported allegations about Gibsonian research. Allocate effort primarily to constructive UD theorems, counterexamples, and predictions. Revisions follow the Agile hypothesis lifecycle; preserve prior provenance and rejected alternatives.
