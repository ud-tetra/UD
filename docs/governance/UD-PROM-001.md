# UD-PROM-001 — Physical Promotion and Research Freedom

Version: 1.0 — proposed 2026-11-23; effective only after accepted review.
Physical Promotion = 0 (unchanged).

## 1. Definition and non-obstruction
Physical Promotion = 0 is the absence of applicable explicit certification of a physical correspondence, not an embargo on mathematical research, physical interpretation, candidate mechanisms or predictions. Missing certification alone must not be used to reject or halt useful research. Identify the missing evidence and next discriminant instead. This doctrine does not waive mathematical consistency, source provenance, applicable no-go results, or repository-specific eligibility rules.

## 2. Research authorization
Encourage forward structural derivations, conditional laws, candidate constants and dynamics, computational experiments, alternative assumptions and test designs. Unknown physical maps may be stated as OPEN or assumed for conditional work. Publish appropriately labeled candidates without implying certification. Mainline integration is not promotion; Discovery may additionally explore inverse design, target backsolving and fitting under its own rules. A failed model may be revised with changed assumptions and preserved failure history.

## 3. Claim typing
Separate EXACT mathematical identities, DERIVED/CONDITIONAL consequences, HYPOTHESIS/CANDIDATE_BRIDGE physical mappings, FIT target-informed selections, NUMERICAL calculations, EMPIRICAL observations, NO-GO/FAILED results and OPEN gaps. Multiple labels may describe distinct parts of a result. Neither proof, numerical proximity, merge, CI nor review alone establishes a physical bridge. Use concise local labels instead of repetitive disclaimers when scope is clear.

## 4. Target exposure
Encourage native forward and parameter-free constructions. A known measured target does not automatically make a derivation a fit; a parameter-free expression does not prove target blindness. Disclose exposed targets, selection history, free choices, formula searches, calibration, assumptions, alternatives and failures. Label target-informed choices FIT; later exact algebra or successful validation does not erase their provenance. Discovery does not require preregistration to explore. Independent prediction requires a frozen evaluation rule and genuinely unused evidence.

## 5. Promotion receipt
Certification is correspondence-specific and requires an explicit applicable promotion receipt with construction and source revisions, dependencies, physical mapping, observable, units/normalization, calibration and target exposure, frozen evaluation protocol, predicted outcome and uncertainty, discriminating alternatives, failure criteria, independent empirical evaluation and review, and exact certified scope. Hardware testing is necessary where the claim requires hardware-specific evidence; no single apparatus is a universal promotion gate. An analog simulator test alone does not certify a claim about nature. Appropriate held-out observations or independent experiments may qualify subject to review. No certification implies universal ontology or automatic promotion of dependent claims.

## 6. Repository boundary
Mainline UD and UD-Physics share this certification boundary while preserving their distinct research admission and export rules. Discovery fits remain permitted and labeled; no automatic mainline export or status change. Existing freezes and no-go results retain their stated scope. This amendment changes governance clarity only; no scientific result, constant, physical mapping, or promotion status is upgraded.
