# UD-MISSION-002 v0.2 — Computational modeling first

**Authority:** Architect-authorized mission realignment, 2026-11-23. **Status:** Authorized direction; repository integration requires reviewed PR. **Physical Promotion = 0.**

## Mission
Unified Dynamics is a computational research architecture for representing, visualizing/rendering, simulating and predicting physical systems with finite geometric and relational models. Its primary tests are mathematical consistency, reproducible computation, controlled conservation, numerical stability and predictive performance against conventional baselines.

**Public line:** “We model the universe digitally. We do not assume the universe is digital.”

The origin as a computing/rendering investigation is architect testimony, not independently verified historical provenance. The framework need not be an ontological theory of everything.

## Ordered research lanes
1. **Computational (primary):** typed data structures; exact incidence, metrics, evolution, rendering, algorithms, errors, convergence, robustness and benchmarks.
2. **Empirical bridge (optional):** mathematical readout mapped to measurement with units, calibration, uncertainty, conventional null, independent held-out prediction and falsification. Physical Promotion = 0 until a correspondence-specific receipt qualifies.
3. **Ontology (optional hypothesis):** whether nature itself is discrete/tetrahedral is a separate claim requiring evidence. Computational success does not prove it.

## Technical guardrails (partner feedback quality control)
- The filled K4 is a minimal 3-simplex within simplicial 3D cells, NOT a cubic voxel and not a physically established unique carrier.
- Boundary nilpotency, B1 B2 = 0 and B2 B3 = 0, is exact chain algebra, NOT by itself an energy/flux continuity law. Dynamic conservation requires a state, current, metric and boundary conditions.
- A metric-skew Hodge–Dirac generator produces a norm-preserving continuous flow for the declared metric. A numerical time-step method is NOT automatically orthogonal or symplectic. Symplecticity additionally requires an explicit symplectic form and its preservation.
- The 192 = 24 × 8 count is the cardinality of the stipulated exposure/fiber set, NOT evidence of 192 physical particles, a single group orbit, or extra spatial dimensions.
- A discretization does not automatically eliminate constitutive coefficients, geometric and mesh choices, boundary conditions, clock maps, or physical calibration. “Zero parameter” is a branch goal, not an unconditional claim.
- A model routing delay or processing overhead is NOT a derivation of relativistic time dilation without a calibrated, independent physical bridge.
- Rendering or resemblance is not empirical confirmation.

## Evidence contract
Each computational release defines state space, degree/basis/orientation, coefficient field, metrics and units, generator/update, initial/boundary conditions, conserved stocks, selection rules, readout and an independently reproducible implementation. Report mathematical checks, sensitivity, convergence or its failure, complexity, controlled baseline, held-out generalization, free choices and negative results. A chosen target-driven formula remains FIT and should not be relabeled as a derivation.

## Review and public communication
Lead with computational objects and their tests. Reviewers assess correctness, numerical controls and predictive value, not an unasserted literal digital ontology. Framing affords no immunity from legitimate mathematical or scientific criticism.

**30-second explanation:** “I approach physical modeling as a computer and systems engineer. UD uses finite geometric structures to explore how well we can simulate, visualize and predict observed behavior. I am not claiming the universe literally runs this architecture. Its mathematics and practical benchmarks must stand on their own.”
