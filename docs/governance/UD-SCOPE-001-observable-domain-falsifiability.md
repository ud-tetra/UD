# UD-SCOPE-001 — Observable-Domain and Falsifiability Doctrine

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** GOVERNANCE LOCK (research scope; not a physical theorem)  
**Physical promotion:** 0  
**Applies to:** Unified Dynamics foundational exposition, derivation reviews, physical-bridge claims, public communications, and comparisons with competing theories.

## Purpose

Unified Dynamics (UD) primarily seeks an accurate, mathematically coherent, independently falsifiable description of physical phenomena observed in three spatial dimensions. Deriving the origin or emergence of dimensionality from zero-, one-, or two-dimensional primitives is **not** a prerequisite or primary mission.

## Locked scope rules

1. **Empirical domain.** Three spatial dimensions are an empirical modeling starting point. This is not a claim that space's ultimate dimensionality has been derived or that spacetime/time has already been recovered.
2. **Carrier selection.** The filled tetrahedral 3-simplex (the clique complex on K4) is the minimal nondegenerate Euclidean 3-simplex: four affinely independent vertices, six edges, four triangular faces, one 3-cell. The graph K4 alone is not a 3-dimensional topological complex.
3. **Selection boundary.** Simplicial minimality is EXACT mathematics, but does not establish that nature uniquely selects a tetrahedral carrier. Physical necessity and uniqueness remain OPEN.
4. **Algebraic versus spatial dimension.** The 15 nonempty simplices yield a 15-dimensional formal carrier when used as basis elements; this is not 15 spatial dimensions. Likewise, the 192-state construction counts internal states, not spatial dimensions.
5. **Auxiliary constructions.** Primitive, emergent, and higher-dimensional mathematics may be explored when it improves the theory, but no corresponding physical ontology is inferred without an independently testable bridge. Higher-dimensional hypotheses are not inherently unfalsifiable; some can produce measurable consequences.
6. **Claim firewall.** EXACT mathematical identities, DERIVED conditional results, CANDIDATE physical mappings, NUMERICAL comparisons, EMPIRICAL tests, NO-GO results and OPEN problems remain separately labeled.
7. **Falsifiability.** Physical claims must specify observable, measurement protocol, units/normalization and scale where relevant, target-independent prediction, uncertainty, discriminating alternatives and failure criteria. Mathematical lemmas are assessed by proof, not direct experiments.
8. **Target blindness.** Post hoc parameter choices, searched integer formulas, fitted coefficients, and measured inputs must be disclosed. Numerical proximity alone is not a physical derivation.
9. **Symmetry of criticism.** Apply identical derivation and prediction standards to UD and competing theories, including Gibsonian Mathematics. Critique only source-supported equations and claims.
10. **Governance boundary.** Physical Promotion = 0 remains in force. No constants, observables, or physical dimensions are certified by this scope lock.

## Formal minimality statement

For affinely independent vertices in Euclidean space, an n-simplex has n+1 vertices. Hence a nondegenerate 3-simplex requires exactly four vertices. This establishes minimality **within the simplicial class**, not among every conceivable 3-dimensional geometric or topological object.

## Practical review gates

- **S1:** Is the proposed object a formal mathematical structure or an empirical physical claim?
- **S2:** Does the construction require more assumptions than explicitly disclosed?
- **S3:** Is any claimed dimensional emergence essential to the observable prediction? If not, treat it as optional.
- **S4:** Does the physical bridge provide a target-blind, discriminating test?
- **S5:** Are empirical failure conditions documented before physical promotion?

## Public-language baseline

> Unified Dynamics begins with observable three-dimensional physical reality and investigates whether finite tetrahedral geometry can support a predictive mathematical description of its behavior. It does not require the emergence of spatial dimensionality to be established as a prerequisite. The tetrahedron's simplicial minimality is mathematically exact; its status as nature's physical carrier remains a testable hypothesis. Internal algebraic dimensions and state counts are not additional spatial dimensions.
