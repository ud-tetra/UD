# Support UD Research

Public funding brief · Revised 3 October 2026

## Purpose

Unified Dynamics (UD) is Benjamin Walker Mayes's independent research program exploring geometric constraints and admissible dynamics on finite tetrahedral structures, with proposed connections to physics evaluated separately. It began in March 2026 and has received no funding.

Support would provide computing resources and independent mathematical scrutiny. The immediate goal is a bounded body of mathematics and code that qualified researchers can reproduce, challenge and extend. Corrections and unfavorable findings are useful research outcomes.

## Review-ready evidence

UD_REVIEW_REPLAY_v0.1.zip contains the recovered September spectral note, exact-arithmetic verifier, supplemental continuity checks, matrices, results, source provenance and a SHA-256 manifest. The declared A/B/C spectra were reproduced. All 128 frozen A face masks passed continuity checks, and three deliberate-error controls were detected.

These are constructor/self-checks, not independent peer review, novelty assessment or proof-assistant verification. Physical promotion remains 0. The package gives a reviewer a bounded task to quote and assess.

## Deliverables

1. Complete definitions and reproducible calculations for the selected claim.
2. Independent review with corrections, counterexamples and scope limitations recorded.
3. A public reproduction package and review summary, subject to agreed reviewer permissions.

Proposed experiments remain contingent on separate feasibility and measurement review. Equipment support and mathematical review do not promise a physical discovery.

## Ways to contribute

Support may take the form of equipment funding, a scoped review, suitable in-kind computing resources or a referral to relevant independent expertise. Arrangements would specify scope, resources, timing and publication terms. Reviewer payment is for careful work regardless of conclusion; sponsor acknowledgment does not imply scientific endorsement.

No payments are collected on the site. Funding, review and in-kind arrangements require agreed terms; no charitable status or tax-deductibility is represented.
