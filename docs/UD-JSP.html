1. Submission Architecture

The AJP submission is restricted to Layer I physics only (per §31.4 Epistemic Firewall). The manuscript maps UD-Theory.html Parts I–VII + Part X (verification) into AJP REVTeX format. Parts VIII–IX (Retained Existence, Quantum Bridge) are held for a second submission after the physics core is established.

Inclusion ruleA section enters the AJP manuscript if and only if it contains: (1) an explicit equation or derivation, (2) a falsifiable prediction or quantitative comparison, or (3) a structural theorem with proof sketch. Governance, portal design, cognitive mappings, and cross-domain grammar are excluded.
Quarantine rule (§31.4)The following are explicitly excluded from the AJP submission: retained-existence engine (Part VIII), quantum world-selection bridge (Part IX), 12-gate Omega admissibility, cognitive/consciousness conjectures, relational systems dashboards, biology/ecology analogies, cross-domain grammar applications. These are reserved for subsequent publications.

3. Equation Extraction Protocol

Every displayed equation in the AJP manuscript must be extracted from UD-Theory.html and converted from KaTeX inline format to REVTeX \begin{equation} format. The following rules apply:

  1. Fraction-first: All exact ratios stay symbolic (7/192, not 0.03646). Decimals appear only in comparison columns.
  2. Label every displayed equation: Use descriptive labels (\label{corrected-einstein}, not \label{eq7}).
  3. Punctuate equations: AJP requires correct punctuation before and after every displayed equation, treating each as part of a sentence.
  4. No built-up fractions inline: Use slashed form (a/b) in running text; save built-up fractions for displayed equations only.
  5. Use eqnarray for aligned multi-line equations (e.g., the corrected field equation channels).

6. Reference Protocol

AJP uses \begin{thebibliography} with \bibitem (no BibTeX). Article titles in double quotes. Book titles in italics. Include beginning and ending page numbers for all articles. Issue numbers encouraged. Authors: "First M. Last and First M. Last" for two; "First M. Last, First M. Last, and First M. Last" for three; "First M. Last et al." for four+.

AJP citation philosophy"References should be a carefully curated list of resources that will be most useful to readers." Each citation must serve one of three purposes: (1) recognize where an idea was first developed, (2) give readers essential background, (3) point to specific additional information. The paper must make clear WHY each reference is cited. Target: 25–35 references. No padding.
