CONTEXT

The paper presents a framework in which the Standard Model gauge group,
electroweak symmetry breaking, a Yang-Mills mass gap bound, the
fine-structure coupling, the lepton mass hierarchy, quantum entanglement,
the Born rule, and the continuum Einstein-Hilbert action are all derived
from a single combinatorial primitive -- the tetrahedron K4 -- with one
input scale and zero free parameters.

KEY FINDINGS

- All structural constants derived from K4 combinatorics (no fitting)
- SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) from S4 representation theory on faces, edge
  pairs, and vertices, with sin^2(theta_W) = 3/11 at unification
- Yang-Mills mass gap >= 3/pi > 0 proved on the IFS lattice
- Electroweak symmetry breaking from the Z2 ferromagnetic framing vacuum
- Nonlinear fermion equation with derived coupling constant
- Born rule from carrier-viability selection (no measurement axiom)
- 12 zero-parameter predictions, including n_s = 185/192, alpha_0 = 7/960,
  and m_tau/m_mu = 101/6 (0.09% match to measurement)

Both companion papers are cited in the manuscript and available as
