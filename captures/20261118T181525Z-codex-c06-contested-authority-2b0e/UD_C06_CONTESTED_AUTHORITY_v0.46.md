## Exact exclusive-selection criterion

Let a finite relabeling group \(G\) act on complete prepared contexts \(x\) and on contacts. Assume eligibility \(A(x)\) is equivariant and requires one eligible contact for an exclusive award. Write \(H=\{g:gx=x\}\). On the orbit of \(x\), an equivariant deterministic single-contact selection exists if and only if
\[
A(x)\cap \operatorname{Fix}(H)\ne\varnothing .
\]
Necessity follows from \(F(x)=F(hx)=hF(x)\) for \(h\in H\). For sufficiency choose an \(H\)-fixed eligible \(a\) and define \(F(gx)=ga\). Different representatives of the same state differ by \(H\), so this extension is well defined. Exactly one fixed eligible contact makes the selection unique on this orbit. Multiple fixed contacts require a further choice. A wait response is always symmetry-compatible; it does not give exclusive progress.

The obstruction can survive positive odd stock. Choose mean-zero vertex potential \((1,1,-1,-1)\) and the associated nonzero ordinary odd preparation \(A^\mathsf T p\) for unsigned incidence \(A\). This is a scoped prepared-state witness, not a claim that all actual gate states retain this symmetry. A source-defined distinguishing receipt can reduce the stabilizer.
