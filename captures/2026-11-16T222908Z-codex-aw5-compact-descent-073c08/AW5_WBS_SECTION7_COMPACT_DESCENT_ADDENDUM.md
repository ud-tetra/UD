### 7.2. AW5 compact-quotient non-autonomy

**EXACT NO-GO WITHIN THE DECLARED QUOTIENT. Physical Promotion = 0.**

On the filled two-tetrahedron bipyramid there are nine edges and five independent edge cycles. Write $Z_1=\ker B_1$, $Y_2=\operatorname{im}B_2^\mathsf{T}$, and $x=B_2e_{012}$. The real flow has $A^2x=-5x$. In the declared turns convention the edge-only compactification identifies $(x,0)$ with $(0,0)$, while keeping the face coordinate real. At native time $s=\pi/(3\sqrt5)$ the edge difference is $x/2$, so the shared-face turn differs by $\|x\|^2/2=3/2$, or one half modulo one. The Wilson characters are therefore different. This single-mode counterexample suffices without any hypothesis about frequency commensurability.

Any descended dynamics would have to preserve equivalence of real lifts at every time. The propagator fails that criterion for the declared edge quotient. An independently constructed compact dynamics remains an open possibility. For real lifted edge-only initial data the shared-face coboundary oscillates at $\sqrt5$ and has mean zero over its period.
