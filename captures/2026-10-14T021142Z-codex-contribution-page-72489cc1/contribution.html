Ways to contribute

Mathematics

Check, challenge or extend a result

Review definitions, proofs and assumptions in discrete geometry, algebraic topology, spectral operators or dynamical systems. Find counterexamples, repair gaps, simplify arguments or compare a construction with established mathematics.

Science

Assess a proposed physical bridge

Examine observables, units, source equations and measurement assumptions. Help define a falsifiable correspondence or review a proposed protocol against the evidence it would actually provide.

Reproducibility

Replay and improve the code

Independently run a frozen calculation, check exact arithmetic and failure controls, document dependencies, or submit a small code improvement with a reproducible result receipt.

Clarity and context

Improve the research record

Clarify notation, identify related work, supply an exact paper or source equation, review a manuscript section, or help make a bounded result easier for another researcher to inspect.

Volunteer independent peer review

If you are a mathematician or scientist willing to scrutinize one defined claim, your help is welcome. Start with a specific artifact and version, agree a manageable scope, then assess the reasoning on its own merits.

  • Proof review: identify the hypotheses, check each inference and report missing steps or counterexamples.
  • Independent reproduction: record the source version, environment, commands, results and any discrepancy.
  • Scientific assessment: separate established mathematics from a proposed physical interpretation; examine testability, dimensional consistency and the required evidence.

A useful review states what was checked, what remains unresolved and whether a correction is needed. Favorable and unfavorable findings are both valuable. Each review is recorded against its specific scope; the review summary and reviewer attribution are published with the reviewer's agreement. Constructor checks and independent reviews retain separate status.

How to get involved

  1. Choose an artifact or propose a bounded task.
  2. Open a contribution offer with your relevant background, proposed scope and availability.
  3. Agree the scope, deliverable and attribution before beginning a coordinated review.
  4. Return your proof notes, code, result receipt or review report with clear assumptions and limitations.

Contributions can be small and self-contained. Attribution, acknowledgments and any coauthorship are agreed according to the contribution. Referrals to suitable researchers or reviewers are welcome.

Computing, equipment and financial support

Practical contributions may include suitable computing equipment, compute access, research hosting or support for a separately agreed independent review. These are additional ways to help alongside volunteer expertise.

Equipment and paid-review planning estimates

The 3 October 2026 planning brief proposed $6,500 for a research laptop package and $4,000 for a scoped independent mathematical review: $10,500 total. These are preliminary US-dollar estimates, subject to quotations and agreed terms. Suitable in-kind resources reduce the corresponding cash need.

The computing baseline was 64 GB RAM and 2 TB storage; final configuration requires representative workload measurements and a dated quote. The small exact replay does not itself require a GPU.

Contribution and research accountability

UD is an independent program exploring geometric constraints and admissible dynamics on finite tetrahedral structures. Contributions should make a defined claim clearer, more reproducible or better tested. Supporting the project or reviewing one artifact does not confer endorsement of the whole framework.

Mathematical results, candidate physical correspondences and empirical evidence retain distinct status. Corrections and unfavorable findings remain in the research record. Physical promotion remains 0.
