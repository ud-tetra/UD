Definition and scope

Same K=<0123,0124>, f=(5,9,7,2), increasing simplex orientations, A=Bᵀ−B, unit metric. A gate scales the two antisymmetric coefficients of its selected incidence. All gates not selected remain at unit coefficient. The affine generator is A+Σ(gi−1)Li. The mask family is a candidate dynamical extension, not a new topology or chain boundary, and not a source-derived physical admissibility law.

We retain the original cell/shared-face readout from the prior audits and require a single autonomous linear representation valid for arbitrary initial states and every setting/schedule in each family. Because the previous all-face–cell family already forces the 14-dimensional space, starting there preserves minimality. Closure under A and each Li establishes exact evolution for all real parameter settings and chronological products of locally finite piecewise-constant controls. No reset map or hidden-variable eligibility law is included.

Why new information becomes necessary

The 14-dimensional record contains all seven faces, both cells, and five edge circulations. It omits gradient edge modes. Set a vertex potential equal to one at vertex 0 and zero elsewhere, and take its edge gradient B1ᵀφ: this has −1 on the four oriented edges 01,02,03,04 and zero on the others. Its retained 14-coordinate record is zero. Under the fixed generator its retained derivatives are also zero. Closing only 01–012 breaks the cancellation around face 012, whose derivative becomes +1. Negating the gradient yields the same initial retained record but derivative −1. The missing distinction is now predictive.

The last hidden mode

With all edge–face gates available, only the uniform vertex vector remains unobservable: one on each of the five vertices and zero elsewhere. It is annihilated by A and by every controlled face–cell and edge–face rotation. Exact rank 22 therefore identifies its span as the entire remaining kernel.

Controlling even the single vertex–edge incidence 0–01 exposes this uniform mode: its gate perturbation produces a nonzero edge component. The exact common closure becomes dimension 23. This is why the final step reaches the whole register.

Proof method and evidence

Starting with row basis O, iteratively append O A and O Li for every gate. Rational row reduction produces the least common invariant row space. Any common autonomous linear representation retaining the requested outputs must contain it. For every terminal basis, the replay explicitly verifies each intertwiner O A=HA O and O Li=Hi O. The output stores exact reduced generators and witnesses; no floating-point tolerance or sampled approximation is involved.

Run python verify_lower.py with standard Python. All inherited verifiers are included. New assertion counts and hashes are in the manifest and lower_results.json. Meaning for the delta programme

Exact compression is jointly constrained by requested observables, allowed initial states, and allowed interactions. A broad enough gate family can expose every formerly hidden coordinate. Even at dimension 23, locality, sparse matrix operations, event-driven work, and approximation may still offer savings; this audit makes no complexity lower bound or speed claim. Conversely, full closure under arbitrary masks does not imply that actual UD admissibility permits those masks independently.
