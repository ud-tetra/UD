Fixed assumptions

Same 23-coordinate real register on <0123,0124>, increasing simplex orientations, unit metric, A=Bᵀ−B. Each candidate gate multiplies both antisymmetric entries of one face–cell coupling by a real parameter. Binary values mean off/on. Why the minimum is exact

For each gate family, repeatedly append O A and O Li to the current readout row space O, where Li is each controlled skew incidence rotation. Stabilization gives the least common invariant row space containing the six-coordinate readout. Any common exact autonomous linear representation of that readout, valid for arbitrary real initial states and every gate setting, must contain all these rows. The saved matrices verify O A=HA O and O Li=Hi O exactly with rational arithmetic.

The model has the full affine control form A+Σ(gi−1)Li; closure under A and every Li proves closure for all real gate parameters. It also proves preservation under chronological products for prescribed locally finite piecewise-constant switching. A held gate mode and decision schedule must still be supplied or represented. State-dependent eligibility must factor through the record; arbitrary reset maps require a separate test. These claims do not establish a minimum for nonlinear encodings, restricted initial states, or a single special schedule.

What the fourteen coordinates mean

The common space can be represented by all seven face amplitudes, both cell amplitudes, and five independent edge circulations. The seven oriented face-boundary circulations have rank five. This yields 7+2+5=14 and no vertex coordinates are needed for these outputs under this particular gate family. Nine dimensions of the original register remain unobserved by these outputs for these dynamics. They have not been declared physically absent.

The ten-coordinate record contains the individual outer-face amplitude c013 and both cell amplitudes. Thus a proposed eligibility predicate based on c013 q1 can be evaluated from it. The fourteen-coordinate record similarly supplies every face–cell product and gated face–cell support current. It does not guarantee eligibility closure for rules reading individual omitted edges, vertices, or external information.

Implication

There is no universal six-coordinate compression. The sufficient record depends jointly on the desired observables and the permitted interaction family. Adding controllable interactions can activate distinctions hidden by the original reduction. Here the required state grows from 6 to 10 to 14, while remaining below 23. Coordinate reduction does not by itself prove a speedup, sparse deltas, physical admissibility, or scalable savings.
