01

The evidence ladder.

Internal mathematics

Proof or reproducible exact calculation on a declared carrier.

Candidate correspondence

Observable, measurement, units, calibration and uncertainty specified.

Frozen independent test

Inputs, baseline, exclusions and acceptance rule sealed before target exposure.

Replication and scoped ruling

Independent evaluation supports only the stated correspondence.

The supplied CRL-2 proposal distinguishes proposed, preregistered, executed and replicated measurement-facing objects. It requires a non-constructor review before advancement. The September 14 extension explicitly separates internal execution from measurement-facing CRL-2 execution.
