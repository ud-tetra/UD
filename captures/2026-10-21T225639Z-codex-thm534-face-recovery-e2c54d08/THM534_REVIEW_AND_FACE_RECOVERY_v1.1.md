\[

**Proof.** Subtract the Cayley equation to obtain \(z'-z=\epsilon A_H(z+z')/2\). Use \(\|x'\|^2-\|x\|^2=(x'+x)^{\mathsf T}(x'-x)\). QED.

## R3 — Correct dynamic latent-support and switch accounting

\[

\[

\qquad
\Delta_{\mathrm{dyn}}\mathcal V_H=-\Delta_{\mathrm{dyn}}\mathcal L_H.

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{switch}}\mathcal V

**Proof.** The midpoint edge equation is

## R4 — Exact all-off first-event reachability

Assume all vertex–edge gates are off at the start of an event. Let \(g=B_1^{\mathsf T}x\), and use the enabled higher-grade blocks of R2. The midpoint equations give

\[

\bar f=\frac{f-(\epsilon/2)B_2^{\mathsf T}y+(\epsilon/2)B_3w}{1+\epsilon^2}.
\]

Therefore

\[
y'=b+kB_2f,\qquad
b=y-\frac{\epsilon^2}{2(1+\epsilon^2)}B_2B_2^{\mathsf T}y,
\quad k=\frac{\epsilon}{1+\epsilon^2}.
\]

**Proof.** With \(t=\epsilon/2\), substitute
\(\bar y=y+tB_2\bar f\) and \(\bar w=w-tB_3^{\mathsf T}\bar f\)
in \(\bar f=f-tB_2^{\mathsf T}\bar y+tB_3\bar w\).
The face Hodge identity makes the left coefficient \(1+4t^2\). Substituting into \(y'=y+2tB_2\bar f\) gives the formula; \(B_2B_3=0\) removes w from the edge result. QED.

**Sharp budget criterion.** For fixed x,y,w, and a selectable initial face amplitude constrained by \(\|f\|\le R\), some gate can reopen after this single event if and only if

\[
\max_e\left[g_eb_e+\sqrt2\,|kg_e|R\right]>0.

**Proof.** Every row of the tetrahedron B2 has norm \(\sqrt2\), because every edge has two incident faces with unit incidence magnitude. The maximum of \(g_e[b_e+k(B_2f)_e]\) over the closed radius-R ball is exactly the displayed expression by Cauchy–Schwarz, attained by a signed multiple of that row. Existence of some edge and some face vector is the union of these edgewise conditions; they need not be optimized simultaneously. The strict inequality is required by the original zero-tie rule. QED.

This is a finite support-allocation statement, not a physical work or action budget. It permits choosing f within a fixed declared ball for an existence question; no native preparatory rule selecting that f has been derived. The criterion concerns the **first event only**, not impossibility of recovery after several events at smaller initial face norm.
