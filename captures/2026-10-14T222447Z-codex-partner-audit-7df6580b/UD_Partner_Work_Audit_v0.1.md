## Why was 10 chosen?

In v0.8 §1.2, the complete mathematical role of $\sqrt{10}$ is an example of an irrational number with an exact radical expression and no finite or eventually periodic positional expansion. There is no declared operator, tetrahedral count, metric, state, observable or physical scale selecting 10 there. The example comes from the user's earlier discussion of decimals and supposed fractality. That discussion introduced $\sqrt{10}$ before this partner upload. The manuscript retained the example without explaining its purely illustrative role.

The criticism is justified: it looks like an unexplained UD constant. The number 10 was editorially incidental. Replacing it with any suitable nonsquare integer would leave that paragraph's argument unchanged. Decimal notation does not select the argument of a square root in a physical theory. Nor do irrational digits establish a fractal universe, stochastic digits, microscopic discreteness or information loss in an exact symbolic description.

Version 0.9 instead uses $\sqrt3$, because the declared 23-coordinate two-tetrahedron carrier has characteristic polynomial $\lambda(\lambda^2+3)^4(\lambda^2+5)^7$. The positive squared frequency 3 actually generates this radical. Its irrationality follows from the parity of the prime-3 valuation of $a^2=3b^2$. This links the example to the subsequent recurrence discussion without promoting a model frequency into a universal physical constant.

The partner document's starting quantity is different:

\[
\sqrt{4/3}=2/\sqrt3\approx1.1547005,
\qquad \sqrt2\approx1.4142136.
\]

These are not equal. Neither expression supports a universal scale law merely because a similar number occurs elsewhere. Likewise a stationary population ratio $4/3$ does not derive a geometric coframe factor unless the channel rates, state-to-geometry map and admissibility of that identification are independently established. No global find-and-replace of literal 10 was made: genuine polynomial coefficients remain.

## Verified additions and their significance

**Trajectory preservation is more selective than snapshot compatibility.** The inclusion defect has rank four, yet its full-trajectory vulnerable space has dimension eight. A kernel at one instant is insufficient. The protected 15-dimensional sector is an exact example of how one geometry change can preserve some whole trajectories while changing others. The result strengthens the narrative about what persistence requires. It neither admits a native event nor supplies its cost.

**A no-go is only as broad as its metric contract.** The stellar map fixes boundary chains and has a forced norm mismatch under unit metrics. Diagonal weighting cannot repair adjoint intertwining. However the chain retraction $\Pi$ gives a reducing decomposition and the exact positive metric $H=\Pi^T\Pi+(I-S\Pi)^T(I-S\Pi)$, which does intertwine the weighted flow. This identifies the real frontier: whether the allowed metric class is local, diagonal, correlated or dynamically selected. It prevents a false universal obstruction from becoming governance.

**A stationary ratio is a property of a chosen channel.** In the two-state example the ratio is simply reverse-rate/forward-rate. Choosing $2/3$ and $1/2$ yields $4/3$. The three-state branch changes it to $(4/3)\alpha$. Those equations demonstrate sensitivity to the channel rather than native selection of a universal constant. They are useful operational examples of the difference between formation by a supplied mechanism and formation derived from UD.

**Population convergence can hide slow coherence.** The population polarization relaxes at magnitude $1/6$ per application while the real coherence relaxes at about $0.986$. Tests confined to diagonal states would substantially overstate retained-distinguishability loss. Opposite $\sigma_x$ states are an exact counterwitness to the imported $5/7$ bound. The corrected result directly supports the manuscript's joint-state and readout discipline.

**Incommensurability is reversible.** The new recurrence proof prevents the absence of exact common periods from being mistaken for entropy production. Record formation still needs its declared apparatus or open dynamics. A channel representation alone does not establish bath renewal or complete energy cost.
