# Separate nested-ball exclusion review

A separate nested-ball exclusion is valid without monotonicity: an eligible exact failure at amplitude a_fail has per-vector noise norm a_fail·2^-48 and belongs to every larger encoded noise ball at the same anchor. Consequently uniform strict-frame success is excluded for every radius delta≥a_fail·2^-48 for the frozen estimator. The smallest failed tested amplitude is only the strongest of these finite exclusions, not a global continuous threshold. A successful smaller tested amplitude gives no uniform success ball.
