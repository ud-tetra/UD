## 2. A declared reciprocal extension

Let c be the real 2640-coordinate oriented chain state of the 600-cell boundary, with dimensions (120,720,1200,600). Let A=A(a1,a2,a3) be its skew Hodge–Dirac generator, with upper blocks -a1 B1,-a2 B2,-a3 B3 and transposed lower blocks. The recovered normalized source model is

\[
c'=Ac+\gamma I_0z,\qquad
z'=-\gamma I_0^Tc-Rz,
\qquad R=R^T\succeq0.
\]

For a fixed positive invertible N, the raw burden equation is

\[
b'=-\gamma N^{-1}I_0^Tc-N^{-1}RNb.
\]

The forward raw map uses N and the reverse uses its inverse. For scalar N=nu I, the two positive cross coefficients are gamma nu and gamma/nu, so their product is gamma squared. A normalization cannot be silently reused as a symmetric raw coupling rate. The spectrum below is independent of N because changing to z is a similarity transformation. Thus that spectrum alone cannot determine the absolute burden normalization or establish the capacity ratio 7/768 as a load threshold.

Define mathematical storage

\[
H=\tfrac12\|c\|^2+\tfrac12\|z\|^2
=\tfrac12\|c\|^2+\tfrac12 b^TN^TNb.
\]

Direct cancellation gives

\[
\boxed{H'=-z^TRz.}
\]

An export account Q'=z^TRz balances H+Q, but is only a storage receipt until a reservoir, energy conversion and renewal mechanism are specified. It does not make the dissipative model a closed microscopic derivation.

**Signed-coordinate obstruction.** Starting from b=0 and a positive vertex support coordinate gives b'=-gamma N^{-1}c0. For positive scalar N and gamma this is negative. Therefore this reciprocal signed coordinate is not automatically a nonnegative burden occupancy. An absolute value, square, density matrix or stock interpretation needs an independently sourced map. Applying the historical capacity comparator directly to this b would skip that obligation.

A finite retention estimate is possible, but indefinite retention of the full original score requires more than passivity. The protected score below supplies that narrower invariant.
