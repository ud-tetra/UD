# UD native gate trigger and recovery: source boundary and conditional derivation v0.1

15 October 2026 UTC. Development note; no registry entry. Constructor checks only; independent review pending. Physical promotion 0.

**Result.** The inspected UD sources supply Boolean transition logic and a historical capacity selector (a_i=\mathbf1[B_i\le7/768]), but no source-complete law making either initiate and restore the preceding 600-cell contact cut. The current ledger scopes the capacity number as a structural ratio and leaves its admissibility law open. A burden-based completion has a precise dynamical test: a closed contact must have a way to repair the failed condition while closed. An isolated self-gated diffusion channel fails that test. Worse, applying the capacity veto to every incident relief edge strands an overloaded vertex even on the 600-cell. Closing only one edge leaves alternate relaxation paths, conditional on a declared diffusion extension. This gives a recovery-time theorem, while the gate mapping, source/repair law and common clock remain open.

## 2. What native factor logic actually derives

For Boolean (C,T), (h=CT). Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
1\to0&\iff (C^-,T^-)=(1,1),\quad (C^+,T^+)\ne(1,1),\\
0\to1&\iff (C^-,T^-)\ne(1,1),\quad (C^+,T^+)=(1,1).
\end{aligned}
\]

These are necessary and sufficient **state-transition conditions**, not a law predicting a transition from current amplitudes or burden. Restoration requires both factors to pass: repairing a putative closure factor cannot reopen the contact while transport integrity still fails.

**Conditional nonidentifiability theorem.** In the theory consisting of the supplied product gate and conditional currents, fix the same initial carrier state and open factors. Both the factor history ((1,1)) forever and a history ((1,1)\to(0,1)\to(1,1)) satisfy the gate definition. Each supplied mask history gives its own well-defined finite skew-flow solution. No source equation in this premise set chooses between them. Thus these premises alone do not entail initiation or restoration. The claim fixes a common initial state, not an identical subsequent amplitude trajectory under different generators. Adding a source-complete predicate evolution would change the premise set and can remove the ambiguity.

Merely writing (C=\mathbf1[b\le b_*]) adds a burden carrier/readout and a factor interpretation; an arbitrary (b_*) also adds an unsourced scalar. The historical capacity selector below does not authorize identifying its site gate with the closure factor (C_e). Even complete hard-factor observations do not determine a quantitative margin: any strictly increasing zero-preserving rescaling gives the same signs and different slopes.

### 2.1 The hard structural condition is stronger than amplitude readiness

The 21 August source defines

\[
C_e=\prod_{\alpha\in\operatorname{Req}_{\rm cl}(e)}r_\alpha^{\rm cl},
\]

where the requirement set is supplied by context and the receipts are already admitted. (T_e) tests a typed edge map (U_{uv}:\mathcal A_u\to\mathcal A_v), its target-domain preservation, required overlaps, and any reverse-map requirement. Nontrivial loop holonomy does not by itself fail either test.

**Retained-sector null theorem.** If the required closure receipts remain present and the declared local transport maps continue to pass all their context tests, (C_e=T_e=h_e=1) throughout, regardless of carrier amplitude redistribution. In the narrower integrity convention (T_e=\mathbf1[U_e^\dagger U_e=I]), exact unitary transport alone fixes (T_e=1). This does not reduce the full domain/overlap integrity predicate to unitarity.

Thus a new amplitude pattern or a rising burden cannot close this structural gate unless a law connects it to a receipt loss or a failed transport test. A separate overload veto can affect an effective coupling, but identifying it with (C_e) or (T_e) needs its own derivation.

### 2.2 Recovered capacity condition, with its current scope

The 11 August capacity-limited source gives

\[
c_*=\frac7{768},\qquad a_i(B_i)=\mathbf1[B_i\le c_*],
\qquad \chi_{\rm hard}(\mathcal B)=\prod_{i\in\mathcal B}a_i.
\]

For this declared selector, a currently passing branch becomes vetoed when any required site's load crosses from (B_i\le c_*) to (B_i>c_*). Its **current-state** site gate passes again when (B_i\le c_*); a whole current-state branch product passes only when every required site passes. Equality is admitted. The exact regression is (a(6/768)=a(7/768)=1), (a(8/768)=0).

The source explicitly says that site re-entry does not resurrect a terminally excluded branch: a later viable branch has new formation identity and provenance. Reopening the preceding stored 600-cell contact is therefore not automatically the same operation as this branch re-entry.

Crucially, the later canonical ledger C03 retains (7/768) as a structural ratio and asks for an independent local admissibility law. The reconciled ledger's authority rule says earlier operational PASS wording does not override later scoped/open statuses. Consequently the historical exact comparison can be replayed, but its native load normalization and physical exclusion interpretation are not promoted here. The capacity integration note does not define (B_i(s)) or derive an evolution driving either crossing.

### 2.3 A capacity-triggered relief gate can strand itself

Suppose, as an explicitly additional identification, (B_i=b_i), and suppose an overloaded site vetoes every incident diffusion contact:

\[
h_{ij}=a_i a_j,\qquad b'=-\kappa B_1\operatorname{diag}(h)B_1^Tb.
\]

If (b_i(0)>c_*), then every (h_{ij}=0), so (b_i'=0) while that inequality holds. It can never reach (c_*) through this evolution. This is an invariant overload, even on a highly connected 600-cell. Alternate paths between its neighbors do not transport burden out of the isolated site. This is a no-go for that particular combined capacity-and-relief rule, not for every UD completion or for using capacity solely to veto new formation.

Recovery must therefore use some process that remains active while the operation is vetoed. For example, **if independently sourced**, a closed-mode law

\[
B_i'=-r(B_i-B_\infty),\quad r>0,\quad B_\infty<c_*,\quad B_i(0)=B_0>c_*
\]

would give the exact site-recovery time

\[
\tau_i=\frac1r\log\frac{B_0-B_\infty}{c_*-B_\infty}.
\]

The solution is (B_i(s)=B_\infty+(B_0-B_\infty)e^{-rs}); substitution proves the crossing and its uniqueness. This formula uses the historical threshold rather than inventing a new one, but (r,B_\infty), the lawful burden observable, its capacity justification, the overload-producing process and the channel repair still need sources. It is a recovery test for a future constitutive submission, not an earned native law. Different sites could recover at different times; a branch still has to satisfy all its other predicates.

## 3. Why readiness is not yet a switch law

For the Paper I two-copy fixed coupling, (y(0)=0) gives (y'(0)=Bv). Recipient stock begins at order (s^2) if (Bv\ne0), at order (s^4) if (Bv=0,B\mathcal Av\ne0), or remains zero when both vanish. Higher-order onset is not a finite waiting interval: a positive leading coefficient gives population for arbitrarily small positive (s). Equal squared amplitudes can have different signed readiness; a simple diagnostic is (B=(1,-1)), with inputs ((1,1)) and ((1,-1)), equal stock and outputs 0 and 2.

The normalized (g_j=R_j/T_F) in §8.2 is a declared candidate response on a supplied contact graph. It provides neither a binary cut trigger for an internal 600-cell edge nor a rule that holds a gate closed and later reopens it. Its contact carrier differs from the 0↔1 incidence contact used in the preceding 600-cell note. No map between those two interfaces is established here. At a symmetric tie a deterministic covariant rule cannot choose exactly one exchanged eligible contact without extra state, a history or a random source. Readiness alone also does not supply that source.

## 4. Burden recovery theorem: isolated contact versus alternate paths

We now declare a **conditional extension**, not a native trigger. Let (b\in\mathbb R^{120}) be a separate vertex burden field with equal unit capacities, no sources, and continuous dimensionless parameter (s). Identify its evolution with the graph diffusion lane

\[
j=-\kappa H B_1^Tb,\qquad b'=B_1j=-\kappa L_Hb,
\qquad L_H=B_1HB_1^T,\quad \kappa>0.
\]

The historical value (\kappa=1/4) is available only within its conditional attempt convention. The ledger's balance variable (\rho), its (b), and carrier amplitude (x) cannot be equated merely by using the same word “burden.” Equal capacities and the stated evolution are explicit premises of this extension. In particular, (x_0=e_u) does not prepare or determine (b_0).

For edge (e=(u,v)), write (d_e=B_1e_e) and signed difference (z=d_e^Tb). Then

\[
z'=-\kappa d_e^TL_Hb.
\]

**Isolated-channel obstruction.** If the sole available channel joins these two vertices, (z'=-2\kappa h_e z). Closing it sets (z'=0). A proposed close-above-(Q), restore-below-(\ell) rule, with (0<\ell<Q), freezes a closed overload above (\ell). No restoration occurs. A fixed closed-mode waiting time would be an additional clock rule; it is not obtained from this current.

**Alternate-path recovery.** For the 600-cell, leave all other contacts open and close only (e):

\[
L_c=L-d_ed_e^T,\qquad L=B_1B_1^T.
\]

The graph remains connected. Its exact vertex-Laplacian characteristic polynomial, reconstructed directly from the 120×120 integer matrix, is

\[
\chi_L(X)=X[(X-10)^2-20]^9[(X-9)^2-45]^4
(X-9)^{16}(X-14)^{36}(X-15)^{16}(X-12)^{25}.
\]

Hence (\lambda_2(L)=9-3\sqrt5), (\lambda_{\max}(L)=9+3\sqrt5<16). Since (\|d_e\|^2=2), for (w\perp\mathbf1),

\[
w^TL_cw\ge(\lambda_2(L)-2)\|w\|^2
=\beta\|w\|^2,\qquad \beta=7-3\sqrt5>0.
\]

Also (L_c\preceq L\prec16I) on this subspace. Thus

\[
\|b_\perp(s)\|\le e^{-\kappa\beta s}\|b_\perp(0)\|,
\qquad |z(s)|\le\sqrt2e^{-\kappa\beta s}\|b_\perp(0)\|.
\]

For any supplied restoration level (\ell>0), the burden difference eventually lies below it. This proves eventual satisfaction of this **proposed burden condition**, not that either native hard predicate has been repaired. If the failed predicate describes a damaged transport map, scalar diffusion alone says nothing about repairing that map. For arbitrary initial (b), a single edge difference need not decrease monotonically even though total disagreement decays.

An especially useful preparation is

\[
b(0)=\bar b\mathbf1+\frac{q_0}{2}d_e,\qquad q_0>\ell>0.
\]

Choose (\bar b\ge q_0/2) if nonnegative burdens are required. Mean does not affect the difference. Spectral decomposition gives

\[
z(s)=\frac{q_0}{2}d_e^Te^{-\kappa L_cs}d_e
=q_0\sum_r w_r e^{-\kappa\lambda_rs},
\quad w_r\ge0,\quad\sum_rw_r=1,\quad\beta\le\lambda_r<16.
\]

Consequently (z(s)>0) is strictly decreasing to zero. A unique first restoration crossing (z(\tau)=\ell) exists, with

\[
\boxed{\frac{\log(q_0/\ell)}{16\kappa}\le\tau
\le\frac{\log(q_0/\ell)}{\kappa(7-3\sqrt5)}.}
\]

The initial rate is (z'(0)=-11\kappa q_0), since (d_e^TL_cd_e=22). Reopening at this crossing, if the other predicate also passes, is a conditional hybrid rule. The chosen preparation, closure event, thresholds, predicate mapping and simultaneous-event semantics remain premises. The calculation does not show how unforced dynamics first develops the overload.

## 5. Does this restore within the previous selection guarantee?

The previous carrier calculation for (L_{\rm carrier}=A-E_e), unit uniform coefficients, gave

\[
g_J(s)\ge s^2/10-864s^4>0
\quad\text{for }0<s<1/\sqrt{8640}.
\]

Once uniform (A) is restored, these projection-score gaps are conserved. To compare the burden recovery time with this window, **additionally assume a common dimensionless parameter** for the two lanes. The sources do not calibrate that identification. Under it, a sufficient condition for the candidate recovery rule to lie in the certified window is

\[
0<\log(q_0/\ell)<\frac{\kappa(7-3\sqrt5)}{\sqrt{8640}}.
\]

A necessary condition from the lower bound is

\[
\log(q_0/\ell)<\frac{16\kappa}{\sqrt{8640}}.
\]

Neither inequality selects a native threshold: choosing it just to meet the bound would be backsolving. For the explicitly arbitrary test ratio (q_0/\ell=2) and (\kappa=1/4), numerical recovery is (\tau\approx0.2567824806), with rigorous lower bound (\log2/4\approx0.17329). Both exceed the certified window upper endpoint (\approx0.0107583). Therefore this example **cannot use that short-time guarantee**. It does not prove the actual score gap is zero or negative at recovery; that would require a separate computation or bound.

For a certified crossing within a closed interval ([s_-,s_+]) lying inside the positive-gap window, evaluate the lower polynomial throughout that interval to obtain a retained margin. Merely knowing eventual burden relaxation is insufficient to claim the earlier numerical margin at (s=0.01).

## 6. Gate transitions also require an ownership receipt

Our preceding 600-cell contact cut kept all 2640 coordinates and the Euclidean full-state norm. Its skew generator is well-defined on both sides of a switch, so an identity state handover preserves that norm. This is a contact coefficient change, not deletion of an edge or establishment of a new boundary operator.

The ledger's Q167 expression is instead the masked accounting functional

\[
S_{\mu,H}=\|x\|^2+\|Hy\|^2.
\]

If fixed amplitudes are carried across a binary single-edge mask change, its accounting jump is

\[
\Delta S_{\mu,H}=(h_e^+-h_e^-)|y_e|^2.
\]

Closing removes this term from the visible account and reopening adds it. This does not establish physical dissipation or energy cost: it establishes a change in what is counted. A reservoir/hidden-register ownership receipt or a declared state transfer must account for it before a closed resource cycle can be claimed. Full-state conservation and masked-account conservation are different assertions. The current derivation does not erase this distinction.

## 7. What would complete a native law

The next source object must declare a primitive local state (Z), including burden, integrity/closure data and any history, and a predictive evolution/update (Z\mapsto Z^+) or generator. It must define (C_e(Z),T_e(Z)) with exact criteria, rather than inferred mask endpoints. Then two stopping conditions are legitimate consequences:

\[
s_{\rm close}=\inf\{s>s_0:h_e(s^-) =1,\ C_e(s)T_e(s)=0\},
\]
\[
s_{\rm restore}=\inf\{s>s_{\rm close}:C_e(s)=T_e(s)=1\}.
\]

For discrete events replace the continuous infimum by the first admitted index and specify ordering. Without (Z)'s dynamics these are definitions, not predictions. At minimum the proposed law must explain (i) which factor fails; (ii) what continues to evolve during closure and repairs it; (iii) why and when both factors become valid; (iv) how simultaneous eligible contacts are admitted; (v) what is transferred at the switch; and (vi) the time and resource dictionary. No scalar dissipation estimate replaces transport-map repair.

**Disposition:** native initiation/restoration remains OPEN in the inspected source set. Exact progress is the factor-transition characterization, a self-blocking recovery obstruction, a graph-mediated recovery theorem with an explicit 600-cell spectral bound, and a timing compatibility test. The conditional diffusion completion is a testable mathematical candidate, not an earned native mechanism or physical record process.
