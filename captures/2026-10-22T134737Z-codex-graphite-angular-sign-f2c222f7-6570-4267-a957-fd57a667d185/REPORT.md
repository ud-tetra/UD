## Why a broad side-difference plateau is possible

Let `x` be a physical displacement in Å along a held probe and let a *hypothetical* contribution to its projected force be `F_join(x)=B(x-t)_+^2` in eV/Å. Here `t` is the displacement at which one angular triple crosses the join; this is a local surrogate for the C2 angular spline composed with its force prefactor, not a fitted physical law. For the two second-order one-sided derivative formulas used by PR #381, its side difference is exactly

`S(h)=B[6p(0)-4p(h)-4p(-h)+p(2h)+p(-2h)]/(2h)`, where `p(x)=(x-t)_+^2`. This uses precisely `Qplus=-[-3F(0)+4F(h)-F(2h)]/(2h)` and `Qminus=-[3F(0)-4F(-h)+F(-2h)]/(2h)`.

At `t=0`, `S(h)=0` for every `h` in this leading-order model. At `0<|t|<h`, it is `B[2t-(3/2)sign(t)t²/h]`. Hence a *shifted* join can give an almost constant discrepancy for `h>>|t|` and a collapse after the stencil stops crossing it (`h<|t|/2`). The side-difference kernel alone is invariant under jointly reversing `B,t`; it cannot determine the sign of the offset. An ordinary smooth force contribution has a leading side-difference term proportional to `h³` if it has four local derivatives. These are mathematical conditional statements, not proof that this join causes the observed mismatch.

## Archived array check

`analyze.py` validates exact hashes for the potential, source, published `LADDER.json` and both PR #381 raw projected-force arrays. A separate toy-force check evaluates the actual negative-force stencils directly against the kernel for five offsets. It independently reconstructs the seven side differences from raw baseline and four sampled forces at each step. The observed `|S(80 µÅ)|/|S(1.25 µÅ)|` is `47.67` for `21_dir_0` and `96.69` for `21_dir_10024`. A descriptive single-hinge plus cubic-in-`h` fit over all seven *exposed* points gives effective `|t|` values `2.63e-6` and `2.58e-6` Å and corrected `B` values `-15.506` and `-16.785` eV/Å³ under the convention `t>0`; maximum residuals remain `4.12e-6` and `3.66e-6` eV/Å². These fitted offsets are **not measured angle offsets or predictions**: many triples can contribute, all seven points were used, the assumed remainder is only illustrative, and the residuals exceed the original `1e-6` side-agreement scale. `B` is not a UD parameter; reversing both `B` and `t` leaves the side-difference unchanged.
