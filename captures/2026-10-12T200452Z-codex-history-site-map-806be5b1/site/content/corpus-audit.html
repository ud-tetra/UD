Ruling

No surveyed public corpus passes the frozen eight-field source-completeness gate.

The search found several useful near-matches:

  • a physical K4 quantum-walk experiment with vertex-state observables;
  • raw electrical voltage/current event datasets with topology switching;
  • experimental simplicial-complex oscillator time series;
  • auditable photonic cloud-job/count packages;
  • photonic tetrahedron/spinfoam simulations;
  • repeated-syndrome QEC histories.

None exposes the complete native tuple required by the frozen POC-1 predictor:

(K4,c0pre,c1pre,Tinthard,θτ,pre-event history,θhidden+,custody). (K_4,\;c_0^{\mathrm pre},\;c_1^{\mathrm pre},\;T_{\mathrm int}^{\mathrm hard},\;\theta_{\tau},\;\text{pre-event history},\;\theta^+_{\mathrm hidden},\;\text{custody}).

Therefore the lawful execution result is no score. Substituting bus voltages for c0c_0, line currents for c1c_1, switch state for hard Gram-defect integrity, or another binary target for Θ\Theta would create a new correspondence hypothesis after corpus selection.

This does not falsify POC-1. It blocks empirical scoring until an admissible dataset is obtained.

Minimal lawful independent dataset specification

A future external corpus must record, for one fixed K4 carrier and without target-aware reconstruction:

  1. four time-resolved pre-event vertex-chain channels;
  2. six time-resolved independent edge-chain channels;
  3. six hard edge-integrity receipts or the raw transport maps needed to evaluate the frozen hard integrity rule;
  4. the three opposite-edge-pair state at the previous completed event;
  5. source-authenticated pre-event ordering through the first latent-obligation change;
  6. the next completed three-pair endpoint kept hidden until prediction freeze.

The strongest current route to such data is a purpose-built independent acquisition rather than retyping an existing public corpus.
