2. six time-resolved independent edge-chain channels;
3. six hard edge-integrity receipts or the raw transport maps needed to evaluate the frozen hard integrity rule;

5. source-authenticated pre-event ordering through the first latent-obligation change;
6. the next completed three-pair endpoint kept hidden until prediction freeze.

The strongest current route to such data is a purpose-built independent acquisition rather than retyping an existing public corpus.
