## Conditional measurable loss channel

Extend the earlier geometry-fixed split only as a control:

    x'' + A x + a z = f
    z'' + gamma z' + k z + a^T x = 0, gamma>=0.

This gamma is an imposed model rate, not a recovered UD coefficient. The old closed model is gamma=0. Total material energy obeys E'=f^T x'-gamma z'^2. An export register Q'=gamma z'^2 closes its normalized energy receipt; adding that receipt does not derive its physical destination.

With phasors exp(i nu t), eliminating z gives

    D_eff(nu) = A - nu^2 I - a a^T/(k-nu^2+i gamma nu)
    Gamma_eff(nu) = Im D_eff(nu)/nu
                  = a a^T gamma/[(k-nu^2)^2+gamma^2 nu^2].

The identity uses real symmetric A and real a. Gamma_eff is PSD for positive gamma and nu; its rank is one when a is nonzero. For prescribed visible displacement x(t)=Re[X exp(i nu t)], the periodic particular solution has Z=-a^T X/(k-nu^2+i gamma nu), and

    mean drive power = (nu^2/2) X^* Gamma_eff(nu) X
                     = gamma nu^2 |Z|^2/2.

This is both the force/displacement phase loss and the independently counted export in the declared model. It vanishes if the overlap is zero, even with a lossy hidden oscillator. Thus a null measurement does not distinguish no bath from a dark coupling.

For gamma=0 away from k=nu^2, the steady particular solution has zero cycle work. At the undamped hidden resonance there is generally no bounded periodic forced hidden solution; secular energy growth cannot be counted as stationary heat. In a retarded spectral description the conservative response has discrete resonant spectral weight, so it is incorrect to claim that a conservative system has no absorption at any frequency. The off-resonance and bounded-periodic qualifications matter. The full coupled system has its own eigenfrequencies; this prescribed-x calculation does not identify sqrt(k) as a full-system mode.

The limit Gamma_eff(0)=a a^T gamma/k^2 shows precisely the additional loss parameter needed even in this minimal extension. H fixes A,a,k, but it does not fix gamma. Changing gamma leaves the equilibrium stiffness unchanged. Any physically matched conventional model with the same coupling and loss produces the same transfer function. A measured nonzero phase lag can reject the fully tracked closed model under the stated conditions; it does not by itself identify UD.
