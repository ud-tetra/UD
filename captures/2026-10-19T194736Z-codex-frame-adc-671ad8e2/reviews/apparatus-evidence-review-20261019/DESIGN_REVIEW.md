# Actual-byte apparatus-evidence verifier design

Development — no registry entry. Same-team design review. Physical Promotion=0. No real apparatus data, independent calibration evidence or external review has been supplied. This design permits conditional numerical validation of bytes under supplied calibration assumptions; it cannot authenticate those assumptions or qualify an apparatus.

## Minimal frozen bundle

Use JSON and fixed-width integer binaries only; no pickle, archive execution or arbitrary decoder code. Every descriptor names a bundle-relative safe path, byte count, SHA256, dtype, shape, order and units. Reject absolute/traversal paths, symlinks or paths outside the bundle, wrong sizes, duplicate IDs, missing bytes, malformed/negative rationals, nonfinite numeric strings and unsupported versions. A processed-byte streaming hash should bind the exact bytes used in the arithmetic; count all entries and reject trailing or truncated bytes. Files must not be silently skipped or zero-filled.

1. CONTRACT.json pins schema version, lane, expected shapes, preparation/time/channel order, coordinate unit, time convention, raw/predictor dtypes, mismatch statistic/space and predata cap, acquisition/calibration validity domains, no-discard policy, required receipts and predictor/validation lineage rules. The real cap remains OPEN until an actual independent predata contract is supplied. A contract hash is not proof of historical chronology.
2. adc.i32le is a(P,N,C) signed integer array in declared preparation,time,physical-channel order. Production temporal209 requires P=37,C=62 and its declared horizon; small fixtures use a separate explicitly synthetic shape contract. Strict calibrated raw-code ranges and clipping/dropout checks apply to every code. Equality with a saturation endpoint is rejected unless the frozen hardware convention proves otherwise.
3. ticks.i64le contains(P,N) timestamps for simultaneous62-vector acquisitions, with a declared tick unit and reference tick per preparation. If channels are multiplexed, provide per-channel timestamps or a frozen skew bound. Check ordering, range and coverage; reject duplicate/backward ticks or missing timestamp evidence. Timestamps alone do not prove the oscillator's calibration.
4. prediction.i64le is the same model-coordinate array shape, with a fixed positive rational quantum, independently generated by a frozen predictor before scored validation. Hash the model, parameters, preparation operator and predictor provenance. Do not accept a supplied residual norm in place of evaluating these bytes. The predictor must be in the exact corrected-channel order and at a specified model-time grid.
5. CALIBRATION.json supplies exact rational per-channel applied gain/offset, gain-error/offset-error bounds, ADC/noise/processing/filter bounds, calibrated amplitude and code domains, source Lipschitz bounds and timebase/skew uncertainty. Every term has units and a role in a displayed decomposition. Denominators are positive and every bound finite/nonnegative. A zero/unsupported inverse gain or incompatible units is HOLD. Content-addressed independently supplied receipts state validity domain, calibration source/preparation IDs, reference/method, uncertainty kind and chronology assumptions; hashes do not establish their truth.
6. LINEAGE.json names model-fit and calibration preparation/raw dependencies, scored validation preparations and raw ancestors, and any independent validation initialization measurements. Explicit dependency closure is required, not merely disjoint filename IDs. Shared instrument-calibration receipts can be role-qualified; validation raw trajectories must not appear among model-fit/calibration trajectory ancestors. Same-anchor remixes, duplicate raw hashes or fitting initial states/amplitudes from the scored future trace cannot be called independent held-out validation. A supplied lineage DAG is still an assumption about physical origin.

The numerical verifier should support only the frozen decoder it actually implements. For an initial minimal diagonal gain/offset decoder, a crosstalk matrix, filter deconvolution or stateful interpolation is unsupported until implemented and independently bounded. Reject its use; do not quietly approximate it by diagonal gains.

## Deterministic decoder and clock budget

For scalar raw z=g_true*y+b_true+n and applied decoder yhat=(z-bhat)/ghat, require |ghat|>=gmin>0, |y|<=A, |g_true-ghat|<=dg, |b_true-bhat|<=db and |n|<=dn. Then

|yhat-y| <= (dg*A+db+dn)/|ghat| <= (dg*A+db+dn)/gmin.

The ADC quantization half-code allowance belongs in dn unless already included in the independent noise bound. It is not added twice. Add separately justified processing, filter-tail and registration terms in model-coordinate units; deterministic components add by the triangle inequality. Aggregate different scalar errors by sqrt(sum e_i^2), which is a norm calculation, not stochastic independence.

For tick zt and frozen reference tick zref, define calibrated model-time estimate that=ohat+rhat*(zt-zref), rhat>0. A valid time error bound is do+dr*|zt-zref|+tick_resolution_error+channel_skew, after converting the tick/second calibration into model-time units. Registered discrepancy |that-tmodel| can also be charged when prediction is on the frozen nominal grid, provided it is explicitly a registration error. Require a finite clock-rate validity range and a positive admitted true tick scale, e.g.rhat-dr>0. Missing rate/range/time units gives HOLD.

A source derivative envelope L yields a coordinate error L*dt. For the declared209-clock source, one safe vector bound is sum_j|theta_j|sqrt(||A_j||2^2+||B_j||2^2), conditional on its admitted coefficient/amplitude domain. Apparatus signals outside that source model cannot inherit its Lipschitz bound automatically. A clock or preparation deviation being tested as model mismatch must not also be subtracted as acquisition uncertainty. Large timing uncertainty yields a larger mismatch interval/HOLD, not stronger evidence.

For a preparation implementation error dp=||T-That||op relative to a pinned anchor with norm Fanchor, the raw preparation-coordinate error is <=Fanchor*dp. Treat it as a separate Frobenius term if justified and not already in scalar budgets. Neither orthogonality nor a supplied dp proves source excitation or particular individual preparation norms.

Current history index step is1 with its declared two-parity source clocks; historical six-ratio h=1/32 and native1893 normalized1/2/candidate16/33 are separate contracts. A calibrated tick rate does not turn2^-48 or1127*2^-48 into an SI noise tolerance. The larger excitation-family allowance is order-only; it does not replace unchanged normal/frame/phase caps.

## Streamed exact residual and acquisition enclosure

For channel c write ghat=p/q, bhat=r/s and predictor entry m/D. The decoded residual is

((q*(s*z-r)*D)-m*s*p)/(s*p*D).

Use Python/exact integers to accumulate the squared numerator per channel. Sum rational channel totals divided by their squared denominators; this produces the exact Frobenius residual squared R2 without storing corrected arrays and without floating cancellation. A negative gain changes residual sign, not the squared denominator. Guard/validate any fixed-width arithmetic before it occurs; ordinary int64 squares can overflow on valid32-bit ADC differences.

Compute per-scalar acquisition bounds from calibration and timestamps, not an untrusted claimed alpha. Exact rational e_i permits exact A2=sum_i e_i^2. Independent Frobenius terms add to sqrt(A2) by the triangle inequality. Use directed interval square roots, with explicit rational endpoints/precision and HOLD on inconclusive comparisons. A single rational common-denominator implementation may optimize accumulation, but must preserve signs and input bounds.

The true mismatch is enclosed by[max(0,sqrt(R2)-alpha),sqrt(R2)+alpha]. Conditional consistency requires validated upper<=frozen cap; conditional contract contradiction requires validated lower>cap; otherwise HOLD. A contradiction means at least one declared model/calibration/registration assumption fails. It does not locate hidden747 modes, prove physical model inadequacy uniquely or identify a physical frequency. Input invalidity/provenance failure is HOLD before the numerical verdict. Report exact R2, computed error components, residual/mismatch interval and counts, not merely a status.

If testing projected sketch space, additionally pin and implement the exact sketch/channel map and use ||(Yhat-Ytrue)S||F<=kA*alpha_raw, kA=sqrt(738137/4096)>=||S||op. A raw-space residual cap cannot be reused for a projected residual without a declared conversion. A first implementation may deliberately test raw space only and HOLD unsupported projection requests.

## Honest status and alias handling

Return separate fields: evidence/shape/hash checks, numeric conditional mismatch verdict, heldout lineage consistency, unaliased identification status, authenticity status and physical verdict. The physical verdict is always HOLD_UNQUALIFIED in this development verifier. A synthetically complete bundle can test the real byte parser and arithmetic; it is not actual independently calibrated hardware evidence. Authenticity and real chronology remain unverified even if signed-looking receipt strings or supplied independence flags exist.

Two-step multipliers identify omega modulo pi. SI/unaliased identification requires an independently frozen injective physical frequency band, sampling/filter evidence and calibrated clock scale. A boolean assertion cannot be the numerical anti-alias proof. A minimal raw-residual verifier may return HOLD_UNALIASED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED even while conditionally validating mismatch. No2^-49 residual gate is reinstated, and2^-38 is not redefined as an apparatus norm.
