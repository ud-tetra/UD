## Exact lifetime requirement

Let \(\theta(t)=\int_0^t g(s)\,ds\) and \(t_*=\inf\{t:\theta(t)=\pi/2\}\). Suppose an exclusive capability is valid for \(0\le t<L\) and is otherwise unrevoked. Its lifetime covers the first crossing exactly when
\[
\int_0^L g(s)\,ds>\pi/2 .
\]
Continuity and strict monotonicity prove the equivalence to \(t_*<L\). Equality is an expiry/root tie. An implementation can admit completion there only if the boundary policy keeps authority valid for that commit. For a merely nonnegative rate a plateau can yield equality even when the root is reached earlier, so the strict integral equivalence uses the stated hypothesis.

If all trajectories are only assumed to hit in finite time, no common finite lifetime follows. For every proposed \(L>0\), the constant \(g=\pi/(4L)\) reaches the root at \(2L\). These are model times and angles. Prearming before activation is a sufficient design under a persistence premise. A valid grant that becomes available later and remains valid through the root can also serve.

## Invalidations and the candidate invariant

A freely revocable capability and guaranteed full completion on every uninterrupted trace are incompatible when revocation before the first root is allowed. If an authoritative veto or expiry occurs at \(\theta_r<\pi/2\), an immediate reciprocal cut by an independently authorized abort path preserves the current state, with donor stock \(N\cos^2\theta_r\) and archive stock \(N\sin^2\theta_r\). This is a partial abort receipt. If only the grant flag clears and the loader keeps running, the v0.44 urgent domain can stall at the first root. Allowing flow over that boundary incurs the prior late-cut risk. At a simultaneous root and invalidation, equal stored stock under two possible cuts does not identify their event semantics; the precedence and ongoing validity must be declared. A pause-and-resume route would require separately authorized continuation and phase accounting.

A conditional reservation mechanism arms the loader only with an exclusive capability bound to the preparation epoch, both register identities and both forward/adjoint control directions. Ordinary requests cannot consume or replace it before the resolved first root. It persists until the matching completion or an explicit terminal abort. At a verified eligible first root, one atomic transition consumes the capability and cuts both directions with identity amplitude reset. Aborts, old epochs, and publication cannot produce another completion. With the v0.44 reachability, state binding and urgency premises, this retains full \(N\) and gives the \(N/2\) Stage 2 readout. A renewal route needs valid coverage and unambiguous authority across handoffs, including the root. This is a candidate contract; the source warrant for authorization, exclusivity, and persistence remains open.
