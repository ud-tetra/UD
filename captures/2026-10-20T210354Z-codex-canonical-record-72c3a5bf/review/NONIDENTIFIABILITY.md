# Conditional nonidentifiability of acquisition independence

Development — no registry entry. Physical Promotion = 0. This is a logical scope result, not evidence that an actual independent replicate exists.

Fix any reference acquisition history h0. Let admissible candidate histories form a set H, let P(h)=Z be the projected rational matrix available to the screen, and let I(h;h0) be a provenance label distinguishing replay/derivation from independent acquisition relative to h0. **If** the admissible history model contains hR and hI with

\[
P(h_R)=P(h_I),\qquad I(h_R;h_0)\ne I(h_I;h_0),
\]

then no deterministic function of Z can correctly infer I on both histories: equal inputs force equal classifier outputs, while the correct labels differ. The same statement holds for any function of canonical bytes or their fingerprint. Even a hypothetically injective hash of Z would not recover history information absent from Z.

This is conditional. It does not establish that the supplied experiment has generated either history, that physical repetitions always coincide, or that a particular observed record is independent. It identifies the missing evidence: an external acquisition/history receipt or a justified model constraint that excludes one alternative. Neither is supplied by a numeric fingerprint. A catalog match therefore cannot prove replay or reject an otherwise independently evidenced repetition.

Conversely, the finite synthetic one-entry perturbations in this work alter numerical matrices using computation alone, without any acquisition event. They can produce unlisted fingerprints. Those concrete screens demonstrate that UNLISTED is not sufficient evidence of independent acquisition or a fresh preparation. They are synthetic mathematical fixtures, not new physical data.

The identity format types **only numerical matrix shape and exact rational entries**. It does not encode physical units, basis/channel meaning, projection definition or preparation/acquisition history. Equal numerical arrays in different physical/unit/basis contexts are not thereby the same physical object, and catalog matches must not merge their evidence. Coordinate-basis/unit/projection receipts may be listed in a separate development checklist; they are not silently added to or claimed validated by the executable fingerprint schema.

Thus every valid listed/unlisted screen keeps independent_acquisition and fresh_preparation NOT_ESTABLISHED, provenance on HOLD, and numerical spectral acceptance NOT_EVALUATED. History authenticity remains OPEN. Same-team review is not external promotion authority.
