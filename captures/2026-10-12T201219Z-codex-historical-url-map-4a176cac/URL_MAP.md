## Additional discovery performed

Indexed web search returned only the root; stale summaries were not used as current page contents. The dashboard names sixteen concrete artifact filenames and the WBS names three tracker filenames. These names are source references, not download links. For each of nineteen names, two possible placements (UD root and UD/source) were checked, giving thirty-eight bounded candidates. Their placement was a hypothesis and never treated as a recovered URL.

Thirty-seven candidates returned 404. The v0.39R1 reconciled-ledger candidate under source returned HTTP 300 with a server-provided alternative pointing to the already-known v0.45 constitutive receipt-index ledger. The HTTP 300 body is preserved. A common basename does not establish identity, redirect equivalence or permission to replace the older source. The alternative adds no new URL to the confirmed set.

No additional historical page or source was discovered by these checks. Neither the dashboard's historical present=true flags nor the WBS's named tracker files establish public downloadability at guessed locations. Absence at these two checked placements is not global absence, deletion, or proof that the artifact never existed.
