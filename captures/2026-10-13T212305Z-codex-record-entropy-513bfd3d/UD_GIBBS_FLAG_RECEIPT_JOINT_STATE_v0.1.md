\[
\tau_F=\tau_c\otimes\tau_p^{\otimes3},\quad
\tau_r=(1-r)|0\rangle\langle0|+r|1\rangle\langle1|,

\[
c=\frac1{1+e^{\beta\Delta_c}},\qquad
p=\frac1{1+e^{\beta\Delta_p}},\quad\beta=1/(k_BT).

For each receipt apply carrier-to-receipt CNOT, private-flag-to-receipt CNOT, and shared-flag-to-receipt CNOT. They commute during acquisition because they have unchanged controls and target bit flips. Their combined action is

\[

## 2. Derive the joint state

From blank receipts the recorded bits are

\[
S_i=R\oplus C\oplus Z_i,
\qquad E_i=S_i\oplus R=C\oplus Z_i.
\]

Tracing the flag registers gives, conditional on R,

\[
\rho_{S|R}=\sum_{e\in\{0,1\}^3}w_e
|R\mathbf1\oplus e\rangle\langle R\mathbf1\oplus e|,
\]

with k=sum_i e_i and

\[
\boxed{w_e=(1-c)p^k(1-p)^{3-k}
+c\,p^{3-k}(1-p)^k.}
\]

After carrier uncopies, the receipt error-state diagonal is the same w_e on |e>. Equivalently,

\[
\rho_E=(1-c)\tau_p^{\otimes3}
+c\,X^{\otimes3}\tau_p^{\otimes3}X^{\otimes3}.
\]

The eight probabilities are nonnegative and sum to one. This is the apparatus's derived joint error state under the declared Gibbs preparation and interactions. The symbolic form precedes choosing test probabilities.

## 3. Exact moments and majority

Write p_bar for each receipt marginal error, a for each pair joint error, b for the triple joint error. Direct marginalization gives

\[
\bar p=p+c(1-2p),\quad a=p^2+c(1-2p),
\]
\[
b=(1-c)p^3+c(1-p)^3.
\]

Thus the local flag probability p is not the receipt marginal p_bar when c is nonzero. The mean pair covariance is

\[
a-\bar p^2=c(1-c)(1-2p)^2\ge0.
\]

Using the exact majority identity,

\[
m=3a-2b=(3p^2-2p^3)+c[1-2(3p^2-2p^3)].
\]

The checker independently obtains these moments from the full flag-state sum and verifies the earlier sharp bounds

\[
\max(0,2a-\bar p)\le b\le\min(a,1-3\bar p+3a).
\]

In particular the shared perturbation creates a majority-error component that is not quadratically suppressed by independent private flags.
