## 9.5 Response constraints for opposite-edge receipts

On the mean-zero six-edge record, let the pair-even and pair-odd sectors carry squared gains μ+ and μ−. The polarized tetrahedral shear input has squared norm factors 1/2 and 1 when projected onto those sectors. A positive equal response therefore requires μ+=2μ−. For a static energy cI+aL_adj+bL_opp with a,b,c nonnegative and at least one coefficient positive, the gains are c+6a and c+4a+2b. The latter is positive, and 3μ−−2μ+=c+6b≥0. Consequently the gain ratio is at most 3/2, including a=0. This is a no-go for this cone of quadratic energies. Alternate readouts require their own source law.

A separate constant-rate diffusion is autonomous and can yield a different ratio when read after an evolution interval. It supplies a conditional time-dependent mechanism with a separate sampling assumption. A matching-sum record supplies another conditional construction. Both still require a source selection law and an independent allocation of the structural deficit. Physical Promotion = 0.
