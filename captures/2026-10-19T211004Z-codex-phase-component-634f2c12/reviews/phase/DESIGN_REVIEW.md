# Independent phase-qualification audit design

Development — no registry entry. Separate same-team reviewer, not external independent promotion review. Physical Promotion = 0.

Only the four previously saved directional outputs are eligible. Bind PHASE_META, PHASE_PROPOSAL, RUN and source target/policy hashes to the pinned PR240 lineage. No estimator, SVD, new inverse/eigenvector proposal or alignment proposal is to be made. Existing strict frame refusals at amplitudes2/4 remain unchanged.

The audit will independently check exact dyadic emission ties-to-even against the saved binary64 angles. For source frequency omega the intended target is exp(2i·omega), so comparisons are per TWO samples and retain modulo-pi ambiguity in the one-step frequency. Circular angular error is independently evaluated as 2·asin(|sin((emitted−2·omega)/2)|), rather than subtraction of principal arguments followed by winding minimization.

All source target pairwise chord lengths 2·|sin(omega_i−omega_j)| will be recomputed to validate the frozen minimum chord separation. A bijective proposed assignment whose angular errors are strictly less than half that fixed chord separation is uniquely nearest: circular angular separation is at least chord separation, and the triangle inequality excludes every other target. This validates a matching proposal rather than assuming it correct.

Saved complex eigenpair and proposed inverse residuals may be independently recomputed exactly as rational functions of their binary64 entries. A validated inverse norm bound and old gates remain typed diagnostics. Approximate eigenpairs/angles are not silently identified with the exact spectrum of the saved shift T. Qualifying emitted lists does not imply SI frequency identification, resolve aliased branches, prove any uniform ball, or repair a failed strict frame gate.

If the four finite lists qualify despite two frame refusals, the result refutes the blanket implication that strict-frame refusal forces inaccurate emitted phases. It does not show that every frame refusal has good phases or that the frozen frame certificate is unnecessary for its original sufficient-theorem scope.
