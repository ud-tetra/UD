## Adversarial checks

1. Independently regenerate the 24 tetrahedra and their oriented boundary incidence matrices; challenge the rank and Betti computations using exact arithmetic. Do not just reuse the supplied code.
2. Verify the algebraic cocycle c on each edge and the three homologous loops. Challenge orientation signs and the difference of unit-metric harmonic representatives.
3. Check whether any supplied source text, rather than an interpretation, already includes O09 omega=0, a local canonical symplectic map, a kinetic action or a normalized burden-to-phase term. Quote version/row if so.
4. Recalculate both exact pumped phases with g=1, and independently test whether the chosen source j is closed. Reject any homology-invariant source claim if it fails closure or an explicit compatible transfer map.
5. Reproduce the canonical action's first variations, assess compact-angle lifting and windings, and verify the donor work identity. Test g=0 and two choices of I>0 to establish source nonidentifiability.
6. Verify the maximal graph degrees, old-step instability and the all-graph PSD/stochastic proof for M=I-L0/(2dmax). Compare the named four-sector coarse outcomes on both tori.
7. Attempt to construct counterexamples using alternative face subdivision maps, different unit metrics, and alternative homologous loops, without fitting any measured value.

## Decision rule

- **PASS scoped math** only if all independent exact identities and counterexamples survive and every additional assumption is explicitly declared.
- **NO-GO scoped native extension** if the source records still fail to select a kinetic scale or coupling while the candidate family remains nonunique; do not claim an absolute impossibility of future extensions.
- **PHYSICAL PROMOTION = 0** unless a *separate* preregistered, independently tested observable bridge specifies the source, physical units, clock, detector, calibration, uncertainties, baseline, and falsification rule.
