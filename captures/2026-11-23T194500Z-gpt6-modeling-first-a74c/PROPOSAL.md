# UD-MISSION-002 v0.1 — Computational modeling first; ontology optional

**PROPOSED amendment (2026-11-23); not accepted canon. Physical Promotion = 0.**

## Mission

Unified Dynamics is a finite geometric and relational computational framework for **representing, rendering, simulating, and predicting observable physical systems**. Its primary success measures are rigorous mathematics, reproducibility, computational utility, robust generalization and independently evaluated prediction against conventional baselines.

**Public statement:** “We model the universe digitally. We do not assume the universe is digital.”

UD's tetrahedra, 15D chain coordinates, 192-state fiber, transport operators, Pachner moves and model clocks are **mathematical/computational objects**. They are not assertions that nature is literally made of tetrahedra, packets or discrete events. A faithful predictive model need not be a fundamental ontology.

## Three research lanes

1. **Primary: computational modeling.** Define typed carriers, update rules, metrics, boundary conditions, observables, numerical verification, rendering, convergence/stability, algorithmic costs and benchmark performance. A rendered visualization alone is not evidence of physics.
2. **Candidate: physical correspondence.** Explicitly specify mathematical readout -> measured quantity, units, calibration, uncertainty, null models and independent falsifiable prediction. Physical Promotion remains 0 unless a correspondence-specific review and test passes.
3. **Optional: foundational ontology.** Explore whether finite structures describe physical substrate. This is a distinct, testable hypothesis, **not** inferred merely from success in lanes 1 or 2.

## Engineering validation contract

For every modeling release: declare computational state and domain; well-posed evolution/boundaries; conserved quantities and approximation errors; input/output/readout map; reference numerical or analytical solutions; competing conventional method; held-out data; runtime/memory cost; mesh/time-step convergence; sensitivity, uncertainties, failure cases and model-selection freedom. No reverse fitting represented as derivation. Preserve exact algebraic results and narrowed no-go scopes.

## External messaging and review

**Thirty-second introduction:** “I approach physics as a computer and systems engineer. Unified Dynamics is a rigorous attempt to model observable reality using finite geometric structures, with the aim of improving simulations, visualizations and testable predictions. I am not claiming the universe literally runs this architecture. The mathematics, implementation and predictive comparisons must stand on their own.”

**Review contract:** Evaluate mathematical correctness, implementation, reproducibility, benchmark quality and predictive power. Ontological claims should be criticized when actually made, not presumed from computational language. This framing does **not** immunize UD from legitimate scientific scrutiny.
