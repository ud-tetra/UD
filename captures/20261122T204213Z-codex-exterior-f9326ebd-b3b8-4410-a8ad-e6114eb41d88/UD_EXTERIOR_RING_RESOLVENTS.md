# Exact exterior-only PB3 and PB5 memory resolvents

CYCLE-088 (#592) · **Physical Promotion = 0** · Constructor evidence pending independent review.

Use the oriented unit-metric simplicial chain operator on the triangular and pentagonal bipyramids. The exterior sink is the sum of the local Whitney edge matrices `(6I - bbᵀ)/9`, with `b = (1, -1, 1)ᵀ` in each exterior face's increasing edge order. The coupling is raw and the radial sink is zero. This is the exterior benchmark convention from [#592](https://github.com/ud-tetra/UD/issues/592), extending the two-cell control of [#589](https://github.com/ud-tetra/UD/pull/589).

Write $a=Q(\partial-\partial^\mathsf{T})Q$, let $V$ be the exterior sink, and let $B=Q(\partial-\partial^\mathsf{T})U$, where the columns of $U$ are the vertex indicators of the cells. Let $u$ be the all-ones column in cell coordinates and $w=aBu$. The exact rational replay checks
$$
VB=\tfrac43 B,\quad Vw=0,\quad
aB=wu^\mathsf{T}/n,\quad aw=-\Omega_n^2Bu,\quad B^\mathsf{T}w=0,
$$
with $B$ of rank $n$, $w\ne0$, and $u$ an eigenvector of $B^\mathsf{T}B$. Here $\Omega_3^2=24/5$ and $\Omega_5^2=40/7$.

Thus the invariant space generated by the forcing is the orthogonal sum of $B(u^\perp)$ and $\operatorname{span}(Bu,w)$. On the first summand the generator is $-dI$, where $d=4g_{\rm ext}/3$. In the basis $(Bu,w)$ the other summand has matrix
$$
\begin{pmatrix}-d&-\Omega_n^2\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The determinant on the memory-active space is
$$
D_n(z)=(z+d)^{n-1}(z^2+dz+\Omega_n^2).
$$
In orthonormal forcing coordinates, let $P_0$ project onto the normalized collective channel and let $P_\perp=I-P_0$. The Laplace transform of the normalized memory kernel is
$$
\widehat{\widetilde K}_n(z)=-\frac{P_\perp}{z+d}
  -\frac{zP_0}{z^2+dz+\Omega_n^2}.
$$
The quadratic is coprime with its numerator because $\Omega_n^2>0$. The linear factor is distinct from the quadratic roots because the quadratic evaluated at $-d$ equals $\Omega_n^2$. Its multiplicity in the determinant comes from the $n-1$ dimensional residue. The memory-active dimensions are therefore 4 and 6.

At $g_{\rm ext}=4$ the quadratic roots are real and strictly negative. The smaller decay rate comes from the quadratic and has a nonzero residue.

| Carrier | Exact slow rate | Certified rational enclosure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PB3 | $8/3-\sqrt{104/45}$ | $(1.146432766,1.146432767)$ |
| PB5 | $8/3-\sqrt{88/63}$ | $(1.484792986,1.484792987)$ |

For any $g_{\rm ext}>0$ the leading exponential rate is $d/2-\sqrt{\max(0,d^2/4-\Omega_n^2)}$. At critical damping the double pole introduces a polynomial prefactor. These are memory transfer claims. A finite-horizon tolerance bound for the two rings and an error-controlled local-in-time closure remain open.
