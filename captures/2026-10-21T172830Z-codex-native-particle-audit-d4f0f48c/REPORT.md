-A²=4a²P_1+4b²P_2+4c²P_3.

Its characteristic polynomial is lambda*(lambda²+4a²)^3*(lambda²+4b²)^3*(lambda²+4c²). Thus the common-scale-free trace moment

R(A)=Tr[(-A²)²]/Tr[-A²]²=(3a⁴+3b⁴+c⁴)/(2(3a²+3b²+c²)²)

These exact unequal values prove that fixed carrier, unit metric, signed symmetry and skewness alone do not choose a unique normalized spectral shape.
