# UD-PM-PRED08-COH-005 v1.0 — Exact local gauge updates preserve integral winding on a fixed carrier

**Kind:** NO-GO / cohomological conservation theorem. **Proof status:** EXACT_CONDITIONAL / VERIFIED_SCOPED. **Review:** DEFERRED. **Physical promotion:** 0. **Date:** 2026-11-01.

## Objects and source premise

Let `K` be a **fixed** finite connected oriented simplicial complex with `H_1(K;Z) = Z` and primitive cycle `Gamma`. Let `z_t` be an integral closed 1-cochain with `d_1 z_t=0` and period `n_t=<z_t,Gamma>`. Assume evolution steps are *only* integral exact updates `z_{t+1}=z_t+d_0 k_t`, where `k_t` is an integer vertex 0-cochain. These are formal gauge/exact updates, not the full generality of local UD matter transport. Event index `t` is not a calibrated clock.

## Theorem

For every such evolution step, `d_1 z_{t+1}=0` and `n_{t+1}=n_t`. Therefore no sequence of exact 0-cochain updates on a fixed carrier can produce an `n=2 -> 3` sector transition.

**Proof.** Incidence nilpotence yields `d_1 d_0=0`, so closedness is preserved. Since `partial_1 Gamma=0`,

\[
 n_{t+1}-n_t=\langle d_0k_t,\Gamma\rangle
 =\langle k_t,\partial_1\Gamma\rangle=0.
\]

Telescoping proves the result for any finite number of updates. QED.

## Sharpness and permitted alternatives

This no-go requires **exact** updates and fixed cycle/complex. A closed but **nonexact** cocycle increment with primitive integral period 1 changes `n` by one, but cannot be generated by an exact update; it inserts a new cohomology class by hand or through some separately governed global/boundary source. A topology-changing cell-occupancy operation can change the carrier's cohomology and invalidate the fixed-K hypothesis. A nonclosed intermediate configuration, as in the distributed staged source of PRED04, also changes the assumed admissible state evolution. These are new branches, not refutations of this theorem.

The precise distinction matters for PRED05/PRED07: the existence of flat integer `n=2,3` cochains and a cost difference `A(3h)-A(2h)` does **not** by itself construct a legal dynamical transition from one to the other. In addition to a sector-sensitive action, a source-complete transition generator or allowed topology/flux-changing mechanism is required.

## Exact witness and status

For an unfilled oriented triangle with `Gamma=(1,1,1)`, every integer potential `k=(k_0,k_1,k_2)` produces edge update `(k_1-k_0,k_2-k_1,k_0-k_2)` with total period zero, while `z=(1,0,0)` is closed and has period one but is not exact. An independent exhaustive check over `k_i in {-2,-1,0,1,2}` covers 125 formal updates. The topological argument is general and does not depend on this finite check.

An absolute action coefficient cannot be uniquely selected by these incidence identities: any positive `tau` in the PRED07 or PRED08 sector term is consistent with the purely kinematic conservation theorem. A source-derived action unit/metric plus a lawful cohomology-changing generator are distinct missing dependencies. Non-constructor review DEFERRED, physical promotion 0.
