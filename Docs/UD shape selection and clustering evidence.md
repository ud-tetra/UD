## What counts as a shape discovery

Three objects must be recorded independently. An activity pattern is a distribution of chain amplitudes or event counts on a supplied carrier. A cluster is a collection of cells with a recorded incidence structure. A metric shape additionally has lengths, angles and an embedding. A pattern moving across an existing cube does not establish cube formation.

For this programme, “snaps to” means an admitted rule maps a range of different initial states to the same configuration class, or produces attraction toward that class. A cluster formation claim requires an actual creation, deletion, attachment or rearrangement transition, or an explicitly defined emergent activity cluster. These are proposed operational definitions for testing; they are not new UD laws.

A preferred motif must outperform declared alternatives under the same rule and preparation ensemble. Relabeling covariance, robustness to perturbation and independence from a chosen drawing or display threshold are required checks. No geometric ranking, damping, energy minimization or attachment preference is introduced merely to obtain a desired shape.
